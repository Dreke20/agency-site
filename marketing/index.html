How much does monthly marketing cost?

Starter (Local Visibility) is $450/mo plus $250 one-time setup. Growth (Starter + Ads) is $850/mo plus $400 setup. Full (Starter + Ads + Social) is $1,350/mo plus $400 setup. Social Content, as a standalone, is $600/mo with no setup fee. Lead-Gen Ads, as a standalone, is $500/mo plus $300 setup. All marketing packages have a 3-month minimum, then continue month to month. Retainers are billed in advance, and setup fees are due at signing.

Starter (Local Visibility)

$450/mo + $250 one-time setup

  • Full Google Business Profile cleanup: categories, services, hours, photos, and Q&A
  • Matching name, address, and phone across Yelp, BBB, Apple Maps, Bing, and Facebook
  • 4 GBP posts per month and a monthly photo upload
  • Review-request link or QR, a script for asking customers, and replies to every review
  • A simple 1-page site or landing page at setup if you don’t have a working site
  • Monthly report: calls, direction requests, and review count

No social and no ads in this package. 3-month minimum.

Growth (Starter + Ads)

$850/mo + $400 one-time setup

  • Everything in Starter
  • Google Local Services or Search and Meta ads management
  • Account and campaign build, call tracking, and a conversion-focused landing page
  • Weekly tweaks and a monthly lead report

Ad spend is separate and paid by you directly. 3-month minimum.

Full (Starter + Ads + Social)

$1,350/mo + $400 one-time setup

  • Everything in Growth
  • Facebook and Instagram: 12 posts a month
  • 2 short vertical videos a month, made from phone footage you supply
  • Light replies to comments and DMs during business hours

Ad spend is separate and paid by you directly. 3-month minimum.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is not included in any package. You pay Google or Meta directly. We recommend at least $750/mo in ad spend. If spend goes above $3,500/mo, the ads management fee becomes 15% of the amount above $3,500.

Do marketing packages promise more leads or rankings?

No. No marketing package promises rankings, leads, or ad results. We’ll do the work listed in the package and report on it clearly.

You can add Search Growth SEO to Starter, Growth, or Full. See also our same-day Google Business Profile fix-up if you only need a one-time listing cleanup.
