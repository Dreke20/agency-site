How much does a website cost?

Website Essentials is a one-time $1,200 for five pages, typically 2–3 weeks. Website Pro is a one-time $2,400 for up to 12 pages, typically 4–6 weeks. Website Care is $79/mo, month to month. Optional extras include an extra page for $125, full copywriting for $75/page, an on-site phone photo session in Macon or Warner Robins (about an hour, edited) for $150, and change requests outside scope at $75/hr, approved in writing first. You pay for the domain and for hosting if the site needs it.

Website Essentials

$1,200 One-time · 5 pages · 2–3 weeks

  • Five mobile-first pages: Home, About, Services, Reviews or Gallery, and Contact, with click-to-call and a Google Map
  • Quote or contact form that emails the owner, and a sticky call button on mobile
  • Basic on-page SEO: titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, LocalBusiness schema, XML sitemap, and robots.txt
  • Google Analytics 4, Search Console, and Bing Webmaster setup in your name, plus tracking for form submissions and call clicks
  • Light copy edit of text you supply, or text from a 20-minute interview, up to about 150 words per page
  • 1 design round and 1 revision round, 30 days of post-launch fixes, and a full handover of logins

Payment: 50% at signing ($600) and 50% at launch ($600). Net 7. The 2–3 week timeline starts when we have your logo, photos, service list, and hours, and pauses while we wait on content. Active Starter clients upgrading from their 1-page site get $150 off ($1,050).

Website Pro

$2,400 One-time · up to 12 pages · 4–6 weeks

  • Everything in Essentials, up to 12 pages total
  • Up to 6 service pages and up to 3 service-area pages, each with unique content
  • Booking embed or a multi-step quote form, plus an FAQ page with FAQ schema
  • Keyword map and internal linking plan, from free tools
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals pass, and 301 redirects from the old site (up to 25 URLs)
  • 2 revision rounds, 30 days of post-launch fixes, and a recorded 15-minute “how to edit” walkthrough on WordPress builds

Payment: 40% at signing ($960), 30% at design or homepage approval ($720), and 30% at launch ($720). Net 7. Timeline is 4–6 weeks from receiving content.

Website Care

$79/mo Month to month

  • Hosting, DNS, and SSL management on your account, plus uptime monitoring and monthly backups
  • On WordPress: plugin, theme, and core updates, and a check that the site still works
  • A monthly form test to confirm you receive the lead
  • Up to 30 minutes of small edits a month (hours, prices, photos, a promo banner). Unused time doesn’t roll over
  • A quarterly speed check and a short health note

The first 30 days after launch are free (the post-launch fix window), then month to month with 30 days’ notice. Extra edits are $75/hr. Care is included free with Search Growth SEO. Not included: redesigns, new pages, or recovery of a hacked site that wasn’t on Care.
