How much does an app cost?

Book & Go is a fixed $4,500, typically about 5–6 weeks. Membership Pro is a fixed $9,500, typically about 9–10 weeks. A Book & Go to Membership Pro upgrade is $5,500 if you upgrade within 6 months of launch. Care Basic is $175/mo for 2 hours of small changes. Care Plus is $325/mo for 5 hours. App Store Publishing is included with Membership Pro, optional $300 with Book & Go, or $450 flat as a standalone for an app we didn’t build or a later relaunch. Extra care work and extra resubmissions are $75/hr, quoted before they start.

Book & Go

$4,500 Fixed · about 5–6 weeks

  • Branded app for iOS and Android, plus a mobile web version
  • Customer sign-up, saved vehicles or service details, and service address
  • Service menu with packages and add-ons, and multi-item quotes at booking
  • Booking calendar with your availability, travel buffers, and a service-area check
  • Stripe Checkout for a deposit or full payment at booking (one-time payments)
  • Automated email and push reminders, plus a follow-up to book again
  • Simple owner admin for the day’s jobs, the customer list, and completed work
  • Basic store listings submitted to the App Store and Google Play, with code and accounts handed off
  • 2 rounds of feedback

Payment: 50% at signing ($2,250), 25% on approval of the working test build ($1,125), and 25% at launch ($1,125). Net 7. Timeline depends on your feedback and app-store review times.

Membership Pro

$9,500 Fixed · about 9–10 weeks

  • Everything in Book & Go
  • Memberships with recurring billing on Stripe Billing, including auto-charge, failed-payment retries, and receipts
  • Multi-item discount tiers for one-time bookings and plans
  • Customer self-service to pause, skip, reschedule, change a card, or cancel, with rules you set
  • Auto-scheduling of plan visits, with an owner approval queue
  • SMS reminders (usage billed to you at cost)
  • Fuller admin: member list, daily route list, revenue and MRR, plan editor, discount codes, and CSV export
  • Before-and-after photo upload per job, shared with the customer
  • The full App Store Publishing add-on is included, with no change to the $9,500 price
  • 3 rounds of feedback

Payment: 40% at signing ($3,800), 30% on approval of the core build with live test payments ($2,850), and 30% at launch ($2,850). Net 7.

What’s included in Book & Go and Membership Pro?

Book & Go includes a branded app for iOS and Android plus a mobile web version, customer sign-up, a service menu with packages and add-ons, a booking calendar, Stripe Checkout for a deposit or full payment at booking, automated email and push reminders, simple owner admin, basic store listings submitted to the App Store and Google Play, and 2 rounds of feedback. Membership Pro includes everything in Book & Go, plus memberships with recurring billing on Stripe Billing, multi-item discount tiers, customer self-service to pause, skip, reschedule, change a card, or cancel, auto-scheduling of plan visits, SMS reminders (usage billed to you at cost), fuller admin, before-and-after photo upload per job, the full App Store Publishing add-on with no change to the $9,500 price, and 3 rounds of feedback.

How does app payment work?

App invoices are Net 7. Book & Go is 50% at signing ($2,250), 25% on approval of the working test build ($1,125), and 25% at launch ($1,125). Membership Pro is 40% at signing ($3,800), 30% on approval of the core build with live test payments ($2,850), and 30% at launch ($2,850). Standalone App Store Publishing is 50% to start and 50% when the apps are live, or after the resubmission limit is used. You supply the logo, photos, and service or plan prices. You pay Apple Developer Program ($99/year) and Google Play ($25 one-time) directly, in your name. Stripe processing fees, SMS or email usage, a custom domain, and hosting without a care plan are also paid by you.

  • Care Basic: $175/mo for 2 hours of small changes, plus Supabase Pro hosting and backups, updates, bug fixes, uptime monitoring, and app-store compliance updates
  • Care Plus: $325/mo for 5 hours, everything in Basic, a monthly usage and revenue report, 1-business-day priority response, and one small feature per quarter within those hours
  • Unused care hours don’t roll over. Extra work is $75/hr, quoted before it starts. Care is optional; you own the code once it’s paid for
  • App Store Publishing (full done-for-you listings, privacy forms, submission, and up to 2 resubmissions per store): included with Membership Pro; optional $300 with Book & Go (basic submission is already in that tier); $450 flat as a standalone for an app we didn’t build or a later relaunch. We don’t guarantee bookings, members, revenue, or app-store approval timing.
