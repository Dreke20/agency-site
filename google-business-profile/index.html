How much does a Google Business Profile fix-up cost?

The same-day Google Business Profile fix-up is $99–$199, one-time. The exact price depends on how much your listing needs, and we’ll tell you that price before we start. You pay half up front as a deposit, and the rest when the work is done. It usually takes about an hour.

What’s included in the fix-up?

A one-time, same-day cleanup of your Google Business Profile. The exact work depends on what your listing is missing. It includes:

  • Photos

    Add or update photos on your Google listing so customers can see your business.

  • Hours

    Set or correct the hours people see when they look you up.

  • Description

    Write a clear business description of what you do and where you work.

  • Categories and contact info

    Clean up categories and website or contact details on the listing.

  • A first post

    Publish a first Google post so your listing has a current update.

  • Review replies

    Reply to the reviews already on your listing.

Do you guarantee better Google rankings?

No. We don’t promise Google rankings, and nobody honestly can. The same-day fix-up makes your listing complete and current. It doesn’t guarantee leads, calls, or ad results.
